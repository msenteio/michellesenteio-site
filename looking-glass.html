Looking Glass

I spent eight years at the XR startup Looking Glass Factory directing brand, content, and product across the company’s consumer hardware launches — including the very successful crowdfunding campaigns for the Looking Glass, Looking Glass Portrait, and Looking Glass Go. I prototyped emergent spatial experiences, built immersive game and narrative experiences, produced innovative storytelling and content for social media. The hardware and software I used included but is not limited to: light field photo rail, Depth Kit DSLR SDK, Unreal, Unity, Blender, Maya, Record3D, and countless experimental tools made by the community.

I also made use of my background in radio and commercial modeling, appearing in two launch videos and producing an extensive amount of tutorials to support new community members. Drawing on my background in game design, my work to make this cutting edge technology playful and accessible to anyone and everyone interested in the future of 3D visual technology was deeply successful and integral to the company’s story.
